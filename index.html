Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tidal Client free to use?

Yes! Tidal Client is 100% free and open-source, built by passionate community developers to provide the cleanest Minecraft launching experience.

Does it support mod repositories?

Absolutely. You can search, install, and update mods, resource packs, and shaders directly from both major repositories inside the client interface.

Can I import my existing Minecraft instances?

Tidal Client features a 1-click migration tool that can detect existing installations from standard launchers and import your worlds and configurations instantly.

Are JVM arguments customizable?

Yes, you have full control over memory allocation, garbage collection parameters, and custom optimization arguments per instance.
